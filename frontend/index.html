    PASSENGER GUIDE

    From station to seat.

    1. Find a departure

      Choose your stations and journey date. Each matching train shows its departure time in IST and how many seats are free.

    2. Hold your seat

      Pick an available seat from the coach map. The five-minute timer shows how long that seat is reserved for you.

    3. Collect your ticket

      Confirm your hold and collect a demo PNR. If the reply disappears, your browser can retrieve the confirmation.

    BEFORE YOU BOOK

    Good to know.

    What happens when my seat hold expires?

    The seat becomes available again. Choose an available seat to start a new hold.

    Which date should I use for the demo?

    The planner starts with the first date in the demo timetable. If a search comes up empty, check the scheduled date below the search fields.

    Can I travel using this PNR?

    Tatkal Shield issues mock tickets for the college showcase. For travel you need a ticket from an official booking channel.
